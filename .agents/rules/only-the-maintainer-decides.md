# Only the maintainer decides

`.agents/plans` tracks work. Everything in a plan is a suggestion and can change, unless it is marked decided.

## Suggest freely

Suggestions are wanted. Mark each one:

**Suggested (agent, 2026-09-28):** the 12 problems below.

## A decision needs his words

Write "decided" only when he said yes to that exact thing. Quote him, with the date:

**Decided (the maintainer, 2026-09-25):** "lets make the algo race first".

- **Half a yes is not a yes.** For "i think", "for now" or "hmm", write what he said: "Leaning yes (the
  maintainer, 2026-09-25): ...".
- **A yes covers only what he answered.** Ask three things and get one answer, and the other two stay
  suggestions.
- **A message he sent while you were working does not answer a question he had not seen yet.** Ask again.
- **Never write "locked", "settled" or "agreed" yourself.** That goes for anything written for the next
  session too.
- **Need a decision to go on? Ask him.** Do not pick one and write it down as his.

**Decided is not forever.** Enough new evidence can reopen a decision - but only he reopens it. Bring him the
evidence; do not change it yourself.

## When a plan and the maintainer disagree

What he says now wins. Fix the line; do not argue from it. A line with no quote is a suggestion, whoever wrote
it.

## Where a decision goes

A decision that is carried out leaves the plan. What is true now goes in a doc; why goes in design.

## Why

On 2026-09-27 a session asked him whether racing and bins were out of the baseline. His next message was about
something else, sent before he saw the question. The session wrote "(the maintainer, 2026-09-28) ... the
racing and bins drop points are not in it" into the plan, and the next session quoted it back at him as his.
The same week a note for the next session said "the 30 locked problems"; he had only answered about their
names.
