{%- comment -%} Written for this site, not copied from the other two - theirs carry five live defects and every one of them comes with the file. What is different here, and why, in the order it appears: 1. `description` is front matter on every page and is never derived from the excerpt. Deriving it through a truncating filter is what cut the live homepage snippet mid-word at "and war". The excerpt fallback is for a page that forgot; every page here has a written one. 2. `og:type` is `website`. `article` is wrong for a product page and it was hardcoded. 3. `og:image` and `twitter:image` are on every page, from a site-wide default. Without one, every link pasted into Slack, Reddit or Hacker News renders a grey card - and the announcement is a link pasted into exactly those places. 4. `twitter:card` is `summary`, because og_image is a square logo and a wide card crops a square image. It becomes `summary_large_image` the day a 1200x630 file exists - the two change together. 5. The tag emits with no whitespace inside it. The live site wraps its value in newlines and some scrapers keep them. The separator is " - ", not "|". Every absolute URL comes from `absolute_url`, so `url` in _config.yml is the only line in the build that names the host. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign page_title = site.title -%} {%- if page.seo_title -%} {%- assign page_title = page.seo_title -%} {%- elsif page.title -%} {%- assign page_title = page.title | append: ' - ' | append: site.title -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign canonical_url = page.url | remove: "index.html" | absolute_url -%} {%- assign excerpt_description = page.excerpt | strip_html | normalize_whitespace | strip -%} {%- assign meta_description = page.description | default: excerpt_description | default: site.description | strip_html | normalize_whitespace | strip -%} {%- assign meta_image = page.og_image | default: site.og_image | absolute_url -%} <title>{{ page_title }}
