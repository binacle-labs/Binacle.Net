{%- comment -%} The replace has to happen in an assign tag, not inside the output tag. Liquid's variable parser trips over the closing brace of the {now} placeholder when it sits inside {{ }}. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign now_year = site.time | date: "%Y" -%} {%- assign copyright = site.data.footer.copyright | replace: "{now}", now_year -%}
