{%- comment -%} The exchange card - the most valuable element on the site. One card, one border, one radius. Not two code blocks. No browser chrome, no traffic lights, no tab bar, no fake terminal. Five rows: the ask, the request, a RESPONSE seam, the response, and a plain-English verdict. The seam is what makes it one exchange instead of two blocks, and the verdict row is the whole reason the card is marketing rather than documentation. It is an include and not page markup on purpose. The card appears on all four pages and its payloads must stay real - one _data/exchange.yml and one template means re-running the call updates every page. Inline copies would be re-run in one page and rot in the other three. The JSON goes through markdownify so Rouge marks it up. The classes it emits are in _sass/_code.scss - that file is the two-tone mapping and it is where the colours are decided. No syntax rainbow, and it is the single biggest lever on whether the card reads as marketing or as a docs snippet.
