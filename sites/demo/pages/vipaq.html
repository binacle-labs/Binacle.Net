ViPaq Decoder

The ViPaq Decoder enables you to decode ViPaq-encoded packing data, providing clear, interactive visualizations of container layouts for easy analysis and validation.

  1. 01 PasteA ViPaq string from a Binacle.Net response, or one from Samples.
  2. 02 Add resultIt is drawn in 3D and kept in the list below the view.
  3. 03 Step throughPlay the order the items went in. Drag to turn the view.
