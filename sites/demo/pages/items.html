Everyday Items

Pick a box, add the things that go in it, and see where each one lands. Every item of a kind has the same colour, so you can follow it.

  1. 01 BoxOne of three sizes.
  2. 02 ItemsAdd or take away with + and -. The sizes are fixed.
  3. 03 PackTap a kind in the list to see where it went.
  4. 04 Step throughPlay the order the items went in, or step one at a time. Drag to turn the view.
