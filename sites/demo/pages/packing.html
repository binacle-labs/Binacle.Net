Custom Sizes

Put in your own bins and items, pick an algorithm, and watch Binacle.Net pack them in 3D. Each algorithm searches differently, so the same items can land in different places.

  1. 01 BinsThe boxes you have. Every bin is tried.
  2. 02 ItemsWhat goes in them, with how many of each.
  3. 03 Get resultsOne row per bin: how full it is and what did not fit.
  4. 04 Step throughPlay the order the items went in, or step one at a time. Drag to turn the view.
